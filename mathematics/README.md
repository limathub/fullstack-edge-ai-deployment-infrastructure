## Area

My doctoral research was in number theory and p-adic geometry — specifically **affine
Deligne–Lusztig varieties**, **Shimura varieties**, and the **Langlands Program** that frames
both.

The rough picture: the Langlands Program predicts a correspondence between objects from number
theory (Galois representations) and objects from analysis (automorphic forms). Shimura varieties
are the geometric stage on which that correspondence is realized, and affine Deligne–Lusztig
varieties describe the combinatorial and geometric structure that appears when that stage is
reduced at a prime `p`.
